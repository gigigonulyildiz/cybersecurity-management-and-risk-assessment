# Cybersecurity Management and Risk Assessment

## Executive Summary

Enterprise cybersecurity management and risk assessment project focused on identifying security governance gaps, evaluating regulatory compliance risks, and developing a comprehensive cybersecurity management plan.

Conducted a scenario-based security assessment for a fictional retail and e-commerce organization, focusing on governance, risk, and compliance (GRC), PCI DSS, GDPR, security policies, cybersecurity awareness, incident response, and business continuity.

## Project Overview

Evaluated an existing enterprise cybersecurity program to identify weaknesses in security governance, regulatory compliance, workforce capabilities, employee security awareness, incident response procedures, and disaster recovery preparedness.

The organization operated retail, e-commerce, and distribution services and processed payment card information and customer personal data.

The assessment identified gaps in security policies, payment card protection, privacy safeguards, compliance staffing, employee training, incident response responsibilities, and business continuity planning.

Developed a cybersecurity management plan outlining mitigation strategies, security governance improvements, workforce recommendations, security policy requirements, and operational resilience measures.

## Key Security Findings

The scenario-based security assessment identified several enterprise cybersecurity weaknesses:

- Missing or incomplete security policies for organizational assets and sensitive information
- Insufficient documented procedures for PCI DSS compliance
- Inadequate privacy safeguards for personal information covered by GDPR
- Limited governance, risk, and compliance workforce capabilities
- Inconsistent cybersecurity awareness training across the organization
- Incomplete incident response roles and procedures
- Insufficient business continuity planning for natural disasters
- Weak password management requirements
- Increased exposure to phishing and social engineering
- Inadequate controls for preventing unauthorized access to sensitive information

## Risk Mitigation & Security Management Recommendations

### Security Governance & Regulatory Compliance

- Recommended security policies aligned with PCI DSS and GDPR requirements
- Defined procedures for protecting payment card information and customer personal data
- Recommended improvements to security governance and compliance oversight
- Proposed three specialized GRC positions covering policy planning, legal and regulatory advice, and security control assessment
- Aligned recommended workforce responsibilities with the NICE Workforce Framework

### Security Policies

Developed recommendations for four enterprise security policies:

**Acceptable Use Policy**
- Defined authorized use of company systems, networks, and information
- Addressed unauthorized software and employee security responsibilities

**Mobile Device Policy**
- Recommended device authentication, encryption, and security updates
- Defined lost-device reporting and data protection requirements

**Password Policy**
- Established strong password and account protection requirements
- Recommended multifactor authentication for sensitive systems
- Defined compromised-account response procedures

**Personally Identifiable Information Policy**
- Defined requirements for collecting, accessing, storing, sharing, and deleting personal information
- Recommended least-privilege access and protection of sensitive data
- Addressed GDPR-related privacy obligations

### Cybersecurity Awareness

- Recommended mandatory security awareness training for new employees
- Established annual refresher training requirements
- Defined phishing, social engineering, and password security training topics
- Recommended specialized training for employees handling payment card and personal information
- Proposed phishing simulations and continuous security awareness activities

### Incident Response

Developed an incident response framework covering:

**Preparation**
- Defined incident response roles, responsibilities, and communication requirements

**Detection & Analysis**
- Recommended investigation of alerts, logs, affected systems, and sensitive data exposure

**Containment, Eradication & Recovery**
- Defined procedures for isolating affected systems, removing threats, and restoring operations

**Post-Incident Activity**
- Established incident documentation, lessons learned, and continuous improvement requirements

### Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

Evaluated natural disaster exposure affecting three fictional enterprise distribution facilities:

- California: Earthquake-related infrastructure and operational risks
- Texas: Tornado and severe weather disruption risks
- Florida: Flooding and equipment availability risks

Recommended business continuity improvements involving:

- Business continuity scope and planning
- Business impact analysis
- Critical system and operational recovery prioritization
- Secure data backups and recovery procedures
- Alternative operational arrangements
- Emergency communications
- Management approval and periodic recovery testing
